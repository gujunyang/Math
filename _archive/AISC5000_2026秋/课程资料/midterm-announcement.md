## Announcement on the mid-term for AISC5000A

Dear students,

The midterm exam will take place on **October 29, from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m.** The **venue will be announced to you via email.** The exam will cover the following topics:

### Lectures 3 and 4

- **Lecture 3:** Sections 1.2 and 2.3.4. Questions will focus on conceptual understanding of the topics covered in these sections.
- **Lecture 4:** Sections 5.1 and 5.2. Questions will be similar in style and difficulty to **Q1 and Q4 in HW3**.

### Chapters 2 and 6

- **Sections 2.1–2.4:** The Rules of Probability, Probability Densities, The Gaussian Distribution, and Transformation of Densities. Questions will be similar in style and difficulty to the homework problems.
- **Sections 6.3–6.4:** Deep Networks and Error Functions. Questions will mainly focus on neural-network concepts and motivation, as well as the choice of activation functions and loss functions for different prediction tasks.

### Lectures 1 and 6

- **Basic linear algebra:** Norms, inner products, matrix–vector products, and eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices.
- **Calculus:** Gradients and Hessian matrices of multivariate functions.
- **Section 7.1:** Loss and error surfaces. Should be able to write down the loss given the data
- **Sections 7.2.1, 7.2.2, and 7.3.1:** Batch gradient descent, convergence, and related topics.

Please review the relevant lecture materials, textbook sections, and homework problems in preparation for the exam.
